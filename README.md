## ❓ FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Can I customize the features?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes! AI Masterji is fully customizable. You can choose whether to include a screen or keep it screen-free. Configure it based on your family's preferences and your child's needs.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How does it stay safe?</b></summary>
<br>
AI Masterji uses a walled garden with no cloud data harvesting, no ads, and filtered content. All interactions are monitored for child safety.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What ages is it suitable for?</b></summary>
<br>
Different characters suit different ages (0-10 years). Each companion adapts to your child's developmental stage.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does it need internet?</b></summary>
<br>
WiFi is required for setup and updates, but many features work offline once content is downloaded.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What about parent voice cloning?</b></summary>
<br>
Optional feature that allows the AI to use your voice for comfort when you're away. Completely secure and only stored on the device.
</details>
